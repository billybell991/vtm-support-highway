About

What this is

A central landing page for internal Versaterm tooling — the MRE onboarding course for new hires, plus the support extensions used day-to-day for ticket triage. One bookmark, three doors.

Where it lives

Hosted on the Versaterm internal network. Open these URLs from any office or VPN-connected machine.

Who maintains it

Found a bug?
